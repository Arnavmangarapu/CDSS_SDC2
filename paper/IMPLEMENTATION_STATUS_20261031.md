# Evaluation revision implementation status

31 October 2026. Rollback checkpoint: `39d532b` (`CDSM before independent evaluation improvements`). This work improves evaluation reproducibility and reporting. It does not establish improved model accuracy or clinical validity.

## Implemented

- Tracked single-provider runner, bounded HTTP 429 recovery, daily-quota stop, incremental failed-attempt records, rotated arms, fixed generation settings and rejection of unavailable/degraded retrieval or provider fallback.
- Returned model version and response metadata alongside requested aliases; separate version-drift reporting. Opt-in timing separates model/HTTP, extraction, embedding, search, reranking, synthesis, verification, pacing and recovery waits. Nested stages are not additive.
- Pinned cached embedding-model revision across retrieval and ingestion. No reindexing, new clinical rule changes or database writes were performed.
- Primary diagnosis denominators retain failed/empty planned cases. Paired exact McNemar, Holm adjustment, descriptive paired bootstrap intervals and non-content per-case correctness exports.
- Exposure registry and conservative candidate selection; read-only corpus fingerprints and obvious QA leakage checks; frozen protocol/data/code/configuration checks. Qdrant fingerprints include vectors and payloads. Graph inventory counts do not certify unchanged edges.
- Safety comparators for lexical, deterministic and intended-facts paths; opt-in LLM-only, production extraction with fixed proposal, and full-report modes. Labels and clinical review are never generated by the harness.
- Blinded claim review packets, disagreement retention, separate semantic-support/applicability scoring, and useful coverage alongside incorrect support and abstention. The gate comparison is a fixed-unknown-context component test, not an added general semantic verifier.
- BM25/dense/reranker comparison tooling over one declared corpus, with unavailable metrics where relevance judgments are incomplete.

See `evaluation/INDEPENDENT_EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md` for commands and prerequisites. A draft is deliberately not certified frozen or independently reviewed.
